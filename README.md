Turkey is a historically rich transcontinental destination bridging Europe and Asia, featuring ancient Greco-Roman ruins, dramatic fairy chimneys, and vibrant coastal rivieras. This list is intended for archaeological explorers, cultural historians, coastal travelers, and remote professionals.

## Official & Planning

Essential official government entry portals, tourism boards, e-Visa applications, and weather tracking.

- [GoTürkiye (Official Tourism Portal of Türkiye)](https://goturkiye.com/) - Official national tourism board portal featuring destination highlights, cultural routes, and travel guides.
- [Republic of Türkiye Ministry of Culture and Tourism](https://www.ktb.gov.tr/) - Official government portal managing archaeological sites, museums, and national tourism policies.
- [Republic of Türkiye Electronic Visa System (e-Visa)](https://www.evisa.gov.tr/) - Official government portal for purchasing official electronic travel visas prior to arrival.
- [Turkish State Meteorological Service (MGM)](https://www.mgm.gov.tr/) - Official national meteorological agency providing weather forecasts, seasonal climate updates, and regional warnings.

## Transportation & Rail

Navigating high-speed YHT train networks, intercity coaches, domestic aviation, and urban rapid transit.

- [TCDD Taşımacılık (Turkish State Railways)](https://tcddtasimacilik.gov.tr/) - National railway operator managing YHT high-speed bullet trains connecting Ankara, İstanbul, Konya, and Eskişehir.
- [Turkish Airlines (THY)](https://www.turkishairlines.com/) - National flag carrier operating extensive international routes and domestic connections through İstanbul Airport (IST).
- [Pegasus Airlines](https://www.flypgs.com/) - Major domestic and international low-cost airline providing frequent regional flights across Turkish airports.
- [Metro İstanbul](https://www.metro.istanbul/) - Official rapid transit authority operating the extensive metro, tram, and funicular networks across İstanbul.

## Destinations & Regions

### İstanbul & Marmara Region

- [Visit İstanbul (GoTürkiye Official Guide)](https://goturkiye.com/) - Official municipal visitor guide for exploring the historic Sultanahmet district, Hagia Sophia, Topkapi Palace, and Bosphorus ferries.
- [Turkish Museums Official Portal (Müze Kart)](https://muze.gov.tr/) - Official ticketing and heritage guide for accessing national museums, archaeological ruins, and underground cisterns.

### Cappadocia & Central Anatolia

- [Göreme National Park & Cappadocia Tourism Portal](https://goturkiye.com/) - Official visitor hub for exploring subterranean cities, fairy chimneys, rock-cut churches, and sunrise hot air balloon rides.

### Aegean Coast & Ancient Ruins

- [Ephesus Archaeological Site Official Guide](https://www.ktb.gov.tr/) - Official visitor portal for one of the Mediterranean's best-preserved ancient Roman port cities and the Terrace Houses.
- [Pamukkale & Hierapolis Official Guide](https://www.ktb.gov.tr/) - Visitor portal for exploring the travertine thermal white terraces and ancient spa city ruins.

### Turkish Riviera & Mediterranean Coast

- [Antalya Provincial Culture and Tourism Portal](https://www.ktb.gov.tr/) - Official regional travel guide for exploring the historic Kaleiçi old town, Mediterranean beaches, and the Lycian Way hiking trail.

## Digital Nomad & Remote Work

Resources for long-term travelers and remote professionals taking advantage of Turkey's digital nomad visa framework.

- [Türkiye Digital Nomads Portal (GoTürkiye / Ministry of Culture and Tourism)](https://digitalnomads.goturkiye.com/) - Official government portal detailing digital nomad identification certificates, income requirements, and application procedures.
- [Kolektif House İstanbul](https://kolektifhouse.co/) - Popular premium flexible workspace and entrepreneurial community hub located across major districts in İstanbul.

## Safety & Emergency Services

Practical advice on emergency contact numbers, tourist police assistance, and medical emergency dispatch.

- [112 Emergency Call Center](https://www.icisleri.gov.tr/) - Integrated single emergency hotline dispatching police (Polis), gendarmerie (Jandarma), medical ambulance, and fire rescue services across all provinces.
- [Tourism Police Directorate (Emniyet Genel Müdürlüğü)](https://www.egm.gov.tr/) - Dedicated police units providing security assistance and support for international travelers in major tourism zones.
